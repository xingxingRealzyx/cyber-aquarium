The substrate uses two CC0 photographic PBR scans from Poly Haven.

Ganges River Pebbles by Amal Kumar
https://polyhaven.com/a/ganges_river_pebbles

Gravel Floor photographed by Matterfield and processed by Jenelle van Heerden
https://polyhaven.com/a/gravel_floor

License for both scans
https://polyhaven.com/license

Color, OpenGL normal and packed occlusion/roughness maps are resampled at
different grain sizes, graded and blended into the sand and river gravel.
The side texture combines the same scans into a sediment cross-section.
