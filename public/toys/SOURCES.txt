The rubber, PVC, canvas and copper tiles come from an original material atlas
generated with OpenAI ImageGen. Each cropped tile supplies a tintable color
map, a tangent-space normal map derived from its grain, and a roughness map.
The models supply the rubber dye, printed ring stripes and suit colors.
